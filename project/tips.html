Photography Tips

1. Understand Lighting

Good lighting is the foundation of a great photo. Natural light often works best for beginners.

2. Use the Rule of Thirds

Imagine your frame divided into a 3x3 grid. Place your subject along these lines for better composition.

3. Keep Your Camera Steady

Use both hands or a tripod to avoid blurry images.

4. Experiment

Try different angles and settings to discover your style.
