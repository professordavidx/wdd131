Website Plan: The Art of Photography

Site Name

The Art of Photography

This name was chosen because it clearly represents the focus of the website, which is learning and exploring photography as both a skill and an art form.

Optional domain: artofphotography.com

Site Purpose

The website introduces beginners to photography by explaining basic concepts and showcasing popular styles.

Scenarios

  • What types of photography can I try as a beginner?
  • How can I take better photos?
